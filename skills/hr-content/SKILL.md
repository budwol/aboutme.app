# HR Content

Use this skill when professional profile content, CV text, project descriptions, skills, experience, or application material needs to be written or reviewed.

## Workflow

1. Establish the target role, audience, language, seniority, and intended document or screen before editing.
2. Extract only claims supported by the source data; never invent employers, dates, titles, metrics, responsibilities, qualifications, or outcomes.
3. Lead with relevant impact and evidence, then add methods, tools, and context. Prefer specific verbs and concrete results over generic self-description.
4. Keep wording concise, scannable, inclusive, and consistent across profile, experience, project, PDF, and localized variants.
5. Preserve the author's voice and factual nuance. Flag ambiguity or missing evidence instead of silently filling gaps.
6. Check language, dates, terminology, accessibility, ATS readability, and consistency with the data contract before finishing.

## Boundaries

- Do not make hiring, legal, immigration, compensation, or discrimination decisions.
- Do not rank people or infer protected characteristics.
- Treat personal data as private and avoid exposing it in examples, logs, tests, or generated artifacts.
